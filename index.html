Dynamic Steering Optimization: SAE Supra

Engineered the steering upright geometry to handle extreme cornering forces without catastrophic failure, strictly adhering to SAE rulebook constraints to improve track lap times.

18% Reduction in unsprung mass via generative design.
2.5 Verified Factor of Safety under peak dynamic load.
1.4G Peak lateral acceleration sustained during simulation.

The core challenge was striking the perfect balance between shedding weight to enhance vehicle agility and ensuring absolute structural rigidity under race conditions. A failure in the steering knuckle at 100km/h is not an option.

1. Kinematic & Load Analysis

Calculated maximum braking, cornering, and bump forces using longitudinal and lateral weight transfer equations to establish a baseline worst-case load scenario. This mathematical foundation dictated all subsequent CAD decisions.

2. Material Selection Trade-off

Conducted a comparative analysis between Aluminum 7075-T6 and 6061-T6. Selected 7075-T6 for its vastly superior yield strength (503 MPa), justifying the manufacturing cost premium through vital weight savings.

3. Topology Optimization (ANSYS)

Utilized ANSYS generative design modules to identify and remove non-load-bearing material. I ran 12 specific iterations, observing the stress paths and refining the CAD geometry until we achieved a structure that reduced mass by 18%.

4. FEA Validation & Tolerancing

Validated the final optimized SolidWorks model using Static Structural analysis in ANSYS. Ensured Von Mises stresses remained well below the yield point, finalizing the manufacturing drawings with tight geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) for the CNC machining phase.
